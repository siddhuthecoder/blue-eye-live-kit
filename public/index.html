ABOUT BLUE EYE MEDIA

One connected studio.
Built for ambitious brands.

Blue Eye Media is a Hyderabad-based creative media and marketing studio. We bring brand thinking, identity, content production, social, performance marketing and digital experiences together—so every expression works towards the same commercial goal.

WHY WE EXIST

“Reach can be bought. Relevance has to be earned.”

Brand, content and distribution become stronger when they are shaped by one idea—not managed as separate departments.

HOW WE THINK

Clarity before decoration.

We begin with what people should understand, remember and do. Design, production and media follow that decision.

HOW WE WORK

Strategy, creative, production and growth work together from the first conversation through execution and learning.

Clear information.

Last updated 10 October 2026

Information we collect

When you start a conversation, we collect the details you provide, including your name, company, email, phone number, selected service, preferred consultation time and project brief.

How we use it

We use this information to review your enquiry, schedule and coordinate a consultation, prepare a relevant response and improve our services. We do not sell personal information.

Storage and service providers

Enquiry records are stored in our website database and accessed by authorised team members to respond to your request. We do not use advertising trackers on this website. Our notification provider processes enquiry details only when email notifications are enabled.

Retention

We normally delete unconverted enquiries after 180 days. Active client records may be retained longer where required for business or legal purposes.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete enquiry information by writing to info@blueeyemedia.in.
